# Il Lab dell’8 — Brand notes

## Positioning

**Brand:** Il Lab dell’8  
**Tagline:** La mente in campo · Football Intelligence

The Tactical Eight symbol combines:

- the number 8, the project’s personal identity;
- a simplified football in the upper loop;
- a tactical-performance trajectory in the lower loop.

## Asset usage

- `app_logo.svg`: primary icon on white or light surfaces.
- `app_logo_dark.svg`: icon placed directly on navy or dark surfaces.
- `app_logo.png`: high-resolution transparent fallback.
- `brand_lockup_horizontal.svg`: reports, documents and light presentation covers.
- `brand_lockup_horizontal_dark.svg`: dark report or social headers.
- `social_avatar.svg`: social profile image and square promotional tiles.
- `favicon.ico`: browser tab icon.

Prefer SVG in the app and in vector-friendly documents. Use PNG only where SVG is unsupported.

## Minimum size and spacing

- Minimum digital icon size: **32 × 32 px**.
- Recommended navigation size: **40–48 px**.
- Minimum print size: **12 mm high**.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least **12.5% of its width**.

## Avoid

- Stretching or rotating the mark.
- Changing individual colours outside the approved light and dark versions.
- Adding heavy shadows, outlines or gradients behind the logo.
- Placing the primary mark directly on a dark surface; use `app_logo_dark.svg` instead.
- Adding extra football, chart or tactical icons beside the mark.
